Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

TboTech s.r.o. operates EdgeCDN-X and is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: admin@edgecdnx.com.

This policy covers edgecdnx.com, our documentation, the production portal, the demo portal, and their authentication and API services.

What we collect and why

  • Accounts and sign-in: email address, name, account identifiers, and authentication/session data to create accounts, sign you in, and manage access. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive basic identity information: your Google account ID, email, name, and profile picture, where provided. We store the account information needed by our authentication service. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or Contacts. Google identity data is used only for account access, not advertising or sale. Other enabled sign-in providers, such as GitHub, supply the identity information you authorize.
  • Service operation: project and DNS/CDN configuration you submit, usage metrics, and technical/security logs, which may include IP addresses, request details, and timestamps. We use these to provide, troubleshoot, and protect the service.
  • Billing: Stripe collects payment details, billing name and address, and tax details where applicable. We share project identifiers and subscription choices with Stripe and retain billing identifiers and subscription status. Stripe processes card details; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Support: information you send us, used to respond to your request.

We process data to provide our services under a contract, meet legal and accounting obligations, and pursue legitimate interests in security and reliable operation. Optional analytics storage is based on your consent.

Cookies and website analytics

Authentication uses necessary cookies/browser storage. We also store preferences such as your theme and analytics choice.

We use Google Analytics on edgecdnx.com to understand website use, including visits, link clicks, browser/device information, and approximate location. The Google tag loads on the website; your analytics choice controls analytics storage and optional tracking. You can allow or decline analytics in the banner. To change your choice, clear this site's stored data in your browser and choose again. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out tool.

Sharing and international processing

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, and payments, and Google for website analytics. We share data as needed to provide these functions or comply with law. We do not sell personal data.

Providers may process data outside your country, including outside the EEA; applicable data-protection safeguards are required for such transfers. See Google's Privacy Policy and Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Retention and your choices

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, including security, dispute resolution, and legal/accounting requirements. Deletion requests may therefore not remove records we are legally required to retain.

Email admin@edgecdnx.com to request access, correction, deletion of your account and associated data, restriction, or portability, or to object to processing. These rights apply as provided by law. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing and complain to your local data-protection authority.

You can revoke Google sign-in access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future access but does not itself delete your EdgeCDN-X account; contact us to request deletion.

We will update this page when our practices change and notify users of material changes where required.
