Introducing EdgeCDN-X:

A Cloud-Native, Open-Source Content Delivery Platform

Hi! My name is Tomáš and I’m the founder of TboTech and the lead developer behind EdgeCDN-X. After years of experience in building and managing content delivery networks, I recognized the need for a more flexible and cloud-native solution that empowers teams to take control of their content delivery infrastructure.

We’re excited to share EdgeCDN-X, a powerful open-source content delivery platform designed for speed, scalability, and flexibility. Built on top of Kubernetes and CNCF projects, EdgeCDN-X combines advanced routing, caching, and secure delivery to optimize content delivery across the globe.

Key Highlights:

  • Declarative Deployment & GitOps: Roll out services seamlessly using ArgoCD and Kubernetes CRDs.
  • Intelligent Routing: Geo-aware DNS routing with active health checks, weighted load balancing, and dynamic reconfiguration.
  • High-Performance Caching: NGINX Ingress-based caching engine with easy customization.
  • Secure URLs & S3 Integration: Protect content with signed URLs and integrate with private or public S3 buckets.

EdgeCDN-X is perfect for teams looking for a flexible, cloud-native CDN solution that scales efficiently while maintaining control over content delivery.
