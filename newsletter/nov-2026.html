Hello everyone,

EdgeCDN-X DNS has moved into production. You can now try the hosted service at portal.edgecdnx.com, with a free plan available to get started.

EdgeCDN-X helps teams manage DNS and routing in a Kubernetes-native platform. Configure straightforward DNS answers or use policies such as weighted routing, failover, geolocation, and round-robin. Health checks and location state help determine which nodes are eligible to receive traffic.

EdgeCDN-X DNS is now in production

The production portal provides access to the hosted EdgeCDN-X DNS service, without requiring you to deploy the platform yourself. Start with the free Starter tier and explore the service before choosing a paid plan.
